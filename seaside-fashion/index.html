TITLE: Seaside Fashion Snapshot

Create an ultra-realistic vertical fashion photograph of ONE 28-year-old adult European woman, inspired by a German / Central-Northern European appearance.

SUBJECT

She is approximately 170 cm tall with the proportions of a naturally slender fashion model.

She has a tall, lean, feminine physique with long limbs, relatively slim shoulders and arms, a narrow ribcage, a clearly defined slim waist and a relatively flat natural abdomen. Despite her otherwise slender model-like frame, she has a naturally full and prominent bust with clear three-dimensional volume and noticeable projection relative to her narrow ribcage and waist.

Below the waist, her silhouette remains feminine rather than extremely thin or straight-bodied. She has a naturally shaped pelvis, gently rounded hips, a softly rounded and moderately projecting gluteal contour, and long, lean but not skeletal thighs. She is slender and model-like, but retains natural feminine curves. Do not make her heavily muscular, extremely skinny, broad-waisted or exaggeratedly hourglass-shaped.

Her overall silhouette should read:
slender shoulders and narrow ribcage → naturally full bust → slim defined waist → feminine hips and naturally rounded glutes → long lean legs.

FACE

She has a realistic Central/Northern European appearance rather than an idealized doll-like or AI-generated beauty face.

Her face is slightly elongated and oval with a relatively open forehead, subtly defined cheekbones, naturally lean cheeks, a softly tapered jaw and a moderately narrow chin.

Her eyes are slightly elongated and almond-shaped rather than unusually large or round. Her eyebrows are natural and understated. She has a relatively straight, refined nose of moderate width and natural lips with slightly more fullness in the lower lip.

Keep subtle facial asymmetry, realistic skin texture and ordinary anatomical imperfections. Avoid an excessively symmetrical, airbrushed or plastic-looking face.

HAIR

Medium-long dark-rooted dirty-blonde hair with lighter blonde highlights through the lengths.

No bangs.

Her hair has been exposed to the sea and is slightly wet, creating irregular natural strands and subtle separation rather than perfectly styled salon hair. Some strands are moved naturally by the coastal breeze.

She is casually sweeping her hair back with ONE HAND. Her fingers pass naturally through the hair around the forehead and crown, lifting the hair backward. The gesture should feel spontaneous and relaxed rather than like a rigid studio pose.

SKIN

The tan should remain believable and relatively light rather than becoming deeply brown, orange or artificially bronzed.

Her forehead, cheekbones, nose, neck, collarbones, upper chest, abdomen, arms and thighs show realistic highlights from strong summer sunlight.

Her skin has a subtle moist sheen consistent with being beside the sea, particularly around the forehead, cheeks, neck and upper chest.

Preserve natural pores, fine texture, small tonal variations and realistic skin detail. No waxy skin, plastic smoothing or excessive beauty retouching.

Thin dark navy or nearly black piping runs along the jacket seams as a distinctive graphic detail.

The front zipper is worn substantially open, creating a relaxed open neckline while the garment hangs naturally according to gravity and body position.

Small-to-medium simple gold hoop earrings.

No prominent necklace, handbag, hat or distracting accessories.

The elevated leg is naturally bent at the knee.

Keep realistic weight distribution through the supporting leg, pelvis and spine. Avoid extreme lumbar arching or anatomically impossible hip rotation.

Her raised elbow creates a loose diagonal line through the upper body. The opposite arm remains relaxed beside her body or rests naturally around the waist/hip.

Her shoulders remain relaxed and slightly asymmetrical.

The overall pose should resemble an experienced fashion model casually changing poses during an outdoor shoot rather than someone standing rigidly for a portrait.

Calm, relaxed and slightly aloof editorial expression.

No broad smile and no exaggerated seductive expression.
